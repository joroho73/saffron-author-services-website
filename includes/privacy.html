Information I collect

When you enquire or work with me, I may collect your name, email address, contact details, book title, project requirements, correspondence, billing information and records of the work we agree.

Files you supply may also contain personal information, such as author biographies, acknowledgements, photographs or information about people mentioned in a manuscript. Please only share information needed for the project and which you are entitled to provide.

My hosting provider may record technical information such as IP addresses, requested pages, timestamps and browser information to operate and protect the website. Website analytics are explained below.

How and why I use your information

  • Enquiries, quotes and agreed services: to discuss your requirements and deliver your project. The lawful basis is taking steps at your request before entering a contract, or performing our contract.
  • Business correspondence and security: to manage communications, keep records and protect the website. This is based on legitimate interests in running a reliable, secure business, balanced against your rights.
  • Accounting and legal requirements: to maintain records and meet applicable obligations. The lawful basis is compliance with a legal obligation.
  • Testimonials and portfolio material: where these include your personal information, I will ask for permission before publishing them. Where I rely on consent, you may withdraw it by contacting me.

Providing information is voluntary, but without the details needed to answer an enquiry or complete a project, I may be unable to help. An enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing emails.

Contact form and email

The contact form sends your details to my email inbox so I can respond. Your message is processed by the website hosting and email providers. Please do not use the form to send passwords, payment-card details or sensitive personal information.

Who receives your information?

Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the business. These include Fasthosts for website hosting and Google for email and website analytics.

Project files including manuscripts and images are stored in cloud storage provided by Google Drive and One Drive. Access is limited to me and any collaborators you authorise. I will not share your information with other clients or third parties without your consent, except where required by law.

Where your project involves a publishing platform, printer or another supplier, I will explain what needs to be shared as part of the work. Information may also be shared with professional advisers or public authorities where necessary or legally required. I do not sell your personal information.

International processing

Some providers including Google Drive and One Drive may process information outside the UK. You can contact me for further information about those safeguards.

Website analytics and cookies

This website uses Google Analytics to understand which pages visitors use and which links they click, and to improve the website. Analytics may include device and browser information, referral sources, identifiers and interactions such as a successful enquiry submission. The analytics events configured for the contact form do not include your name, email address or message.

You can read more about Google's handling of information in Google's privacy policy .

File retention and archiving

Please download and safely store the files delivered to you. You are responsible for keeping your own copies of your original materials and completed work.

Working manuscripts, scans and intermediate drafts are normally retained for six months after final delivery or cancellation. Final files and the editable source files needed to amend them are normally retained for two years after final delivery. This does not change which source files are included in your quote.

After these periods, files may be securely deleted. Longer-term storage can be arranged separately in writing. File retention does not include free revisions, updates or an ongoing backup service.

Routine project correspondence is normally retained for two years after completion. Accepted quotes, contracts, approvals and key project decisions are normally retained for six years after completion or termination.

Relevant records may be retained longer where necessary to meet legal obligations or resolve an outstanding complaint, dispute or claim. Further details, including how backups are handled, are set out in the privacy policy .

Keeping information secure

I take reasonable organisational and technical measures to protect personal information and limit access to those who need it. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

If a project involves processing personal information on your behalf, we will agree any additional data-processing arrangements needed before that work begins.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may ask to access or correct your personal information, have it erased, restrict its use or receive a portable copy.

You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests, taking account of your particular circumstances.

Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it at any time. This does not affect processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal. Some information may still need to be retained for other lawful reasons.

To exercise your rights, email saffronauthorservices@gmail.com .

Questions or complaints

Please contact me if you have concerns about your information. You also have the right to complain to the UK's Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk .

Changes to this policy
