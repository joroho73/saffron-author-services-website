A few useful answers

Does my manuscript need to be finished?

You can get in touch at any stage. Final formatting is best done once the text has been edited and proofread, so you’re not paying to lay out pages that will change.

Can you help with just one part?

Yes. You may need an interior formatted, a cover file prepared or help navigating the publishing setup. We can agree a focused piece of work.

Can we work together remotely?

Yes. Files, feedback and publishing decisions can be shared remotely, so you don’t need to be local.

Will I stay in control of my book?

Yes. This is a support service for independent authors. You make the creative and publishing decisions, and we’ll agree how accounts and files are handled at the outset.
