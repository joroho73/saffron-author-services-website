Hello, I’m John

Creative work deserves practical care.

I started Saffron Author Services to help independent authors move from a finished manuscript to a published book with less uncertainty and more confidence.

I’m a writer myself, with a background in technology and project delivery. I enjoy bringing those two sides together: understanding the care that goes into the words, and working through the technical details that help them reach readers.

My approach is straightforward and collaborative. I’ll explain the options in plain English, help you make informed decisions and take care over the work we agree.

Whether you need a book formatted, help preparing a cover or a website for your writing, you’ll work directly with me.
