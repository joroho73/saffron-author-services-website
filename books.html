Fantasy · Fiction

The Fire Phials

JD Connolly

The future… it is not yet written…

At the dawn of creation, two phials are created. One protected by good. The other by evil. Should they ever be joined, the world will be no more.

It is long ago in a world now lost in history. A city is under siege and the King is dead.

I helped John with the paperback formatting, creation of the ebook, building an author website and publishing his book on Amazon and The Great British Bookshop.

Fiction · Western

Voodoo Arizona - Immortal Gunslinger

Simon C Walker

For 150 years, Arizona has wandered the frontier, cursed with immortality by an ancient ritual that bound him to the spirits of the Loa.

Outlaws fear him, legends surround him but behind the gunfighter's infamous reputation lies a man crushed by a lifetime of grief, condemned to watch everyone he loves die.

Simon and I worked together to bring this unique western tale to life, from editing and design to publishing the paperback and ebook on Amazon and The Great British Bookshop.

Fiction · Short Stories

Tales of Trouble

Multiple Authors

Tales of Trouble is a gripping collection of short stories that explore the unexpected twists, moral dilemmas, and darkly human moments lurking beneath everyday life.

These stories explore how small and conflicting perspectives can gather momentum and tip lives into trouble - sometimes with chilling consequences.

I worked with a group of talented authors at a short story workshop at the Fisher Theatre, Bungay to bring this unique collection to life. My work included editing and designing the volume, then preparing the paperback and ebook for publication on Amazon.

Poetry

Pertaining to Rivers

John Bissett

John started writing poetry as a young man. His themes reflected his observations of nature, his work and gardens. He also wrote about the problems of the world, his family and community events.

All the profits from the book are being donated to the Campaign To Protect Rural England to support their work in protecting our rivers and wildlife.

I worked with his daughter Julia to scan all his poems. We used AI to digitise the scans. I then compiled them into a volume, created a cover and published it on Amazon.
